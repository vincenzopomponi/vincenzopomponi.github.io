Research Overview

My research focuses on the intersection of Machine Learning, Generative AI, and Robotics, with particular emphasis on Diffusion Models, Robot Learning, and Learning from Demonstration.

I investigate how generative models can enable robots to generalize across tasks, compose learned behaviors, and perform robust long-horizon manipulation.
