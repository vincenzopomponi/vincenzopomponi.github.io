## Teaching Activities

---

### Diffusion Models — From Mathematical Foundations to Practical Coding

**Politecnico di Milano (PoliMi)**

A lecture series covering the theory and implementation of diffusion models, addressed to students at PoliMi.

Topics covered:

- Forward & reverse diffusion processes
- DDPM & DDIM sampling
- Diffusion architectures
- Mathematical derivations
- Practical Python implementation

Repository: [Chaos2Clarity](https://github.com/vincenzopomponi/chaos2clarity)

---

### Robotic Laboratory — Collaborative Robotics Practical Sessions

**SUPSI**

Hands-on laboratory sessions for students at SUPSI, focused on applied collaborative robotics.

Topics covered:

- Collaborative robot programming
- Robotic manipulation
- Industrial automation
- Safe human–robot interaction
