About Me

I am an external PhD student at Università della Svizzera Italiana (USI) and a researcher at SUPSI ARM Lab.

My work bridges Robotics, Generative AI, and Machine Learning, with a focus on diffusion-based robotic manipulation systems.

I obtained both my Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Mechanical Engineering from Politecnico di Milano.
